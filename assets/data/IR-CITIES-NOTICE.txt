Iranian province, county, and populated-place names in iran-province-cities.json are adapted from:
https://github.com/mekramy/ircity
Pinned upstream revision: 8ba2f09189bbdb8154ffeb5ebffb3ce2c1840f99
Upstream files: province.json, county.json, and city.json
The checkout list combines all 448 county names with 1,354 type-0 populated-place records, deduplicated within each province. Type-2 records (sampled entries are numbered subdivisions, such as “آبادان 1”) are excluded to avoid presenting those subdivisions as separate city choices.
